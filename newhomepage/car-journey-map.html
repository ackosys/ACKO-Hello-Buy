10. Confirm Details

After add-on selection, the system reconfirms key details before moving to the review screen: policy expiry date, inspection requirement, and personal details.

Step 1 — Policy Expiry Date

If the expiry date was auto-fetched or entered during pre-quote, it is shown pre-filled. Otherwise the user must enter it — it's mandatory for continued coverage.

Step 2 — Inspection Requirement

No inspection: Policy not expired or expired ≤ 10 days → show policy start date.
Inspection required: Expired 10–90 days + Comp/ZD/OD plan → schedule via slot picker (< 10 min, crew comes to user).
No inspection needed if the policy is still active or expired within 10 days. Inspection required (under 10 minutes, team comes to the user) if it expired 11–90 days ago and the user chose comprehensive cover.

Step 3 — Personal Details

Name (pre-filled, editable) + Email (always required) + Phone (only if not logged in). Once confirmed → Review Screen.
